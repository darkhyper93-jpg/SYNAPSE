SYNAPSE: Entrena tu mente

**Descripción breve** (80)
Desafíos mentales rápidos que se adaptan a ti. Memoria, lógica, reflejos y más.

**Descripción completa** (4000)
SYNAPSE es un juego de desafíos cognitivos rápidos, variados y **adaptativos**: el juego aprende cómo juegas y ajusta cada reto para mantenerte en el punto justo, ni aburrido ni frustrado.

🧠 12 DESAFÍOS ORIGINALES
• ECHO: memoriza el tablero y recuerda posiciones, colores, secuencias y cambios.
• ONE SECOND: absorbe un tablero lleno de información en menos de un segundo.
• TRACE: sigue esferas que se mueven y se cruzan.
• CONTRADICT: descubre reglas ocultas y detecta qué ejemplo las rompe.
• SPARK: reflejos con control, golpea las chispas y esquiva los señuelos.
• FOCUS: atención selectiva entre distractores que orbitan.
• MIRROR y PARALLAX: rotaciones, reflejos y figuras 3D.
• FLEX: la regla cambia y tú cambias con ella.
• DECISION: decide bien bajo presión.
• DUAL y NEXUS: desafíos combinados que se desbloquean demostrando nivel.

📈 SE ADAPTA A TI

Animaciones fluidas, fondo neuronal interactivo, combos, rachas, estrellas, notas por sesión, celebraciones al subir de nivel y vibración háptica. Cada partida se siente distinta.

🗓️ DESAFÍO DIARIO
Siete desafíos nuevos cada día, los mismos para todos.

📊 TU PERFIL SYNAPSE

Nota: SYNAPSE es un juego. Sus puntuaciones reflejan el rendimiento dentro de sus propias tareas; no es un test de CI ni una herramienta médica.

## English (en-US)

**App name** (30)
SYNAPSE: Brain Challenges

**Short description** (80)
Fast brain challenges that adapt to you. Memory, logic, reflexes and more.

**Full description** (4000)
SYNAPSE is a game of fast, varied and **adaptive** cognitive challenges: it learns how you play and tunes every challenge to keep you in the sweet spot, never bored, never stuck.

🧠 12 ORIGINAL CHALLENGES
• ECHO: memorise the board and recall positions, colours, sequences and changes.
• ONE SECOND: absorb a dense board in under a second.
• TRACE: track orbs as they move and cross.
• CONTRADICT: discover hidden rules and spot the example that breaks them.
• SPARK: reflexes with control. Hit the sparks, dodge the decoys.
• FOCUS: selective attention among orbiting distractors.
• MIRROR & PARALLAX: rotations, reflections and 3D shapes.
• FLEX: the rule changes, and you change with it.
• DECISION: choose well under pressure.
• DUAL & NEXUS: combined challenges you unlock by proving your level.

📈 IT ADAPTS TO YOU
Every skill has its own difficulty. Too easy? It ramps up. Too hard? It eases off. Always in the 70–90% accuracy zone where you improve.

✨ MADE TO BE PLAYED
Smooth animations, an interactive neural background, combos, streaks, stars, session grades, level-up celebrations and haptics. Every game feels different.

🗓️ DAILY CHALLENGE
Seven fresh challenges every day, the same for everyone.

📊 YOUR SYNAPSE PROFILE
An 8-skill radar (memory, perception, logic, reaction, attention, spatial, decision, flexibility), mastery levels, progress charts and history.

⏱️ SHORT SESSIONS, REAL PROGRESS
5 minutes is enough. No ads, no accounts, no artificial rewards: just you beating yourself.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Works offline. Your progress stays on your device. We collect no data.

♿ ACCESSIBLE
Reduced motion, high contrast, text size, colour assist and optional sound.

Note: SYNAPSE is a game. Its scores reflect performance within its own tasks; it is not an IQ test or a medical tool.
